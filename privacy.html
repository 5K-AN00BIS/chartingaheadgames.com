Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers Emberstead: Frozen Frontier ("the game") and chartingaheadgames.com ("the website"). It explains what information they handle, which outside services they use, how long information is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

Who we are

The game and the website are run by Ryan Bachman, trading as Charting Ahead Games ("we", "us"), in Arizona, USA. We are responsible for the information described here. For any privacy question or request, email support@chartingaheadgames.com.

Your game progress

Your save slots are stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your saves can also be stored in your Google Play Games account (Saved Games) so you can continue on another device. Deleting a save slot in the game also deletes its cloud copy. We do not run our own servers and cannot see your saves.

Services the game uses

  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game boost. AdMob can collect and share your device's advertising ID and app set ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, how you use the game (such as app launches, taps and video views) and diagnostics (such as launch time, hang rate and energy use). Google uses this to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. This can happen while the game is running, not only when you watch an ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent through Google's consent message. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat handle the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID so the purchase can be confirmed and restored, and so we can see how purchases are doing. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services provides achievements and cloud saves if you choose to sign in, using your Google Play Games account. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Feedback you send us

The "Send feedback" button opens a Google Form in your browser. It asks for a rating and your comments. It is also pre-filled with the game version, the platform and your progress in the current save (furnace level, workers, zones, play time and current goal) so we can understand your report. An email address is optional and only used to reply to you. Responses are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets in our Google account and are used only to fix and improve the game. We read them there and on a private project page that runs on Anthropic's Claude service.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your address and your message to reply. They are stored in our Google Workspace account.

The website

The website has no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising, cookies or embedded content from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and our Cookie Policy.

What we don't ask for

The game has no accounts of its own, no chat and no analytics service beyond those listed above. It does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, precise location or anything else stored on your device. Please don't put sensitive personal information in your feedback or emails.

Why we use your information

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, these are our legal reasons:

  • Saves, achievements and the Remove Ads purchase: to provide the game and what you bought.
  • Ads: your consent where the law requires it. Otherwise, our legitimate interest in funding a free game.
  • Feedback and emails: our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the game.
  • Website logs: the legitimate interest in keeping the website secure.

You can play without sending us feedback or signing in to Google Play Games. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who receives your information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information goes only to the services named in this policy, and we may share it if the law requires us to. Some laws treat the advertising data described above as "sharing" or a "sale" even when no money changes hands. The Ads choices below let you limit it.

These services may process information in the United States and other countries. Each one explains how it protects information in its own policy.

How we protect information

Information the game sends over the network is encrypted in transit. Feedback and emails are kept in password-protected Google accounts that only we can sign in to. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you when the law requires it.

How long we keep information

  • Saves: until you delete them or uninstall the game. Cloud saves stay in your Google Play Games account until you delete them.
  • Feedback and emails: up to 2 years, then we delete them, unless we need a particular message for longer to settle a dispute or meet a legal duty.
  • Information held by Google, RevenueCat, GitHub and Anthropic: for as long as their own policies say.

Do Not Track

The website does not track you, and no other company tracks you through it, so a browser's Do Not Track signal changes nothing there. The game does not respond to Do Not Track signals. Use the Ads choices below instead.

Children

Emberstead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Ads: ads only play when you choose to watch one. Where consent is required, you can change it at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (Pause → Go Ad-Free) makes the boosts free without ads.
  • Deleting your data: deleting a save slot removes it from your device and its cloud copy. Uninstalling the game removes the saves on your device. You can delete the game's cloud saves in your Google Play Games settings. To delete feedback or emails you sent us, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to correct or delete it, to limit or object to how we use it, to move it to another service, and to withdraw consent at any time. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To use them, email us. The game has no accounts, so please tell us what you sent us and which email address you used, so we can find it. We will answer within one month. For information held by Google or RevenueCat, we will help as far as we can, and you can also contact them directly.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please email us first. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, and tell you the outcome.

You can also complain to the data protection regulator where you live. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In the European Economic Area, see the list of national authorities.

Changes to this policy

If the game or the website starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
