Emberstead Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Emberstead ("the game") is made by Charting Ahead Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, which outside services it uses, and the choices you have.

Your game progress

Your save slots are stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your saves can also be stored in your Google Play Games account (Saved Games) so you can continue on another device. We do not run our own servers and cannot see your saves.

Services the game uses

  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game boost. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device information and an approximate location based on your IP address to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent through Google's consent message. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat handle the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID so the purchase can be restored. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services provides achievements and cloud saves if you choose to sign in. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Feedback you send us

The "Send feedback" button opens a Google Form in your browser. Along with what you type, the form is pre-filled with the game version, the platform and your progress in the current save (furnace level, workers, zones, play time and current goal) so we can understand your report. An email address is optional and only used to reply to you. Responses are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets in our Google account and are used only to fix and improve the game.

What we don't collect

The game has no accounts of its own, no chat and no analytics service beyond those listed above. We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location or anything else stored on your device.

Children

Emberstead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Ads: ads only play when you choose to watch one. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (Pause → Go Ad-Free) makes the boosts free without ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game removes the saves on your device. You can delete the game's cloud saves in your Google Play Games settings. To delete feedback you sent us, email us.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
