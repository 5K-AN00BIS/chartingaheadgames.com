Refund Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Emberstead: Frozen Frontier is free to play. The only thing we sell is the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase, which you buy through Google Play. Google takes the payment, so we never see your payment details.

Within 48 hours of buying

You may be able to get a refund from Google, depending on the details of the purchase. Request a refund from Google Play.

After 48 hours

Email us at support@chartingaheadgames.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA) and tell us what went wrong. We look at every request. If the purchase doesn't work and we can't fix it, we will refund it.

Please don't send us your card or bank details. We don't need them.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom

Google Play's refund rules for your region apply, and they explain when you can cancel a digital purchase. You can also get a remedy if a purchase is faulty or isn't as described.

After a refund

If a refund is issued, you lose access to Remove Ads, and the game's boosts go back to needing a rewarded ad. Your saves aren't affected.

Free in-game items

Coins, boosts and other in-game items aren't bought with real money and can't be refunded or exchanged for money.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under the consumer protection laws where you live. See also Google's refund policies for apps and in-app purchases and our Terms of Service.
