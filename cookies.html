Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

chartingaheadgames.com ("the website") does not use cookies. It also has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, scripts or embedded content from other companies, and it stores nothing on your device beyond what your browser keeps as a normal cache.

Why there's no cookie banner

A cookie banner asks for your permission to store or read information on your device. The website doesn't do that, so there's nothing to ask you about.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

The website links to other sites, such as Google's. Those sites may use cookies once you visit them, under their own policies.

The feedback form

The game's "Send feedback" button opens a Google Form in your browser. Google may use cookies on that page. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The game

Emberstead is an app, so it doesn't use website cookies, but its ads use similar technology, such as your device's advertising ID. Our Privacy Policy explains this. Where consent is required, you can change it in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

Changes to this policy

If the website ever starts using cookies or similar technology, we will update this page first, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
