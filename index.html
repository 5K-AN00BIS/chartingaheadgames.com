Emberstead

Our first game · Android

The valley is frozen and the furnace is nearly out. Lead the last few survivors: chop pines, fish the frozen lake, build up a snowbound camp and hire workers. Keep the fire alive, because a bigger camp needs a bigger fire.

  • One-thumb controls, built for phones
  • A living low-poly camp with day and night, weather and blizzards
  • Workers who lend a hand as your camp grows, and sled expeditions into the storm
  • Free to play, with optional rewarded ads and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase
